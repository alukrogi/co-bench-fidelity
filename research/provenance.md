# Sources and the identity of the runs

The public record contains paper versions, benchmark configurations, framework adapters, public inputs and numerical summaries. A task-level number identifies a reported result. Connecting it to a particular historical search also requires its experiment revision, invocation, selected program and underlying observations.

We inspected the 62-commit history reachable from the pinned main branch, tree and README, the public issues and maintainer replies, release list and tags, the publisher record, the project leaderboard, the versioned articles and the Hugging Face dataset. The GitHub release list and tag list were empty at inspection. We did not locate a score-linked launcher, candidate archive, per-instance test results and classical configurations sufficient to replay Figure 3 end to end. The absence claim is confined to the artifacts examined. A description of a method or a later fix cannot by itself establish which private runner, overrides or files an earlier experiment used. ## How lace-audit informed this work

The private audit studies Gong et al.'s LACE and traces the CO-Bench scores LACE reused. Its baseline notes flagged model settings, selection and the difference between outer steps and model requests. Its CO-Bench study identified case weights, overlapping splits and runtime conventions. Its methodology report traced the EoH aggregate discrepancy downstream into LACE. This audit independently reads Sun et al.'s appendix and release, compares the nine named methods with their upstream mechanisms or stated controls, and adds executable probes, timing runs and a neural-variant comparison. Findings about LACE's maritime cases, portfolios or helper access cannot establish faults in a CO-Bench historical run. [Private methods report](https://github.com/alukrogi/lace-audit/blob/5179d63a14b25689574a0aa10ff2fdd8aace890b/report/methodology-fidelity.md) · [CO-Bench follow-up](https://github.com/alukrogi/lace-audit/blob/5179d63a14b25689574a0aa10ff2fdd8aace890b/report/co-bench-study.md) · [Baseline notes](https://github.com/alukrogi/lace-audit/blob/5179d63a14b25689574a0aa10ff2fdd8aace890b/notes/baselines.md)

We also recomputed the direction of change from lace-audit's stored costs on 128 size-1,000 TSP instances. On its one-trial versus two-trial LKH runs, the latter produced 66 shorter tours and 62 ties. All 66 improvements increase the native reference/length score and decrease the paper's symmetric score. This is real evidence of a scoring reversal on those tours. The LKH executions and witnesses belong to that prior study. We rechecked its arithmetic and directly inspected the CO-Bench normalizer. Its matched 36-task DeepSeek search is a separate comparison at another backbone and budget. Its practices of sharing starts, saving requests, freezing selections, retaining failures and verifying witnesses inform the proposed replication here.
